Explain this result & simulation assumptions + Methodology
Synthetic Population Modeling

Audience cohorts are sampled from curated behavioral archetypes with Gaussian jitter applied to traits (attention, skepticism, digital literacy, language barriers). Each agent possesses personalized conversion thresholds and social connectivity.

Network Topology & Channel Physics

The network is synthesized using homophily clustering and family cliques (3–5 nodes) with high-degree community hubs. Channels modulate transmission: WhatsApp features small-world clustering, SMS uses 1-to-1 spread, Social Feed acts via hub broadcasts, and Call permits zero forwarding.

Hybrid Reaction Engine

Segment responses combine local Qwen2.5-VL LLM reasoning (60%) with deterministic domain logit rules (40%). The model strictly enforces behavioral axioms: action ≤ engagement ≤ notice.

Community Immunity & Interventions

Vigilant agents who report suspicious messages establish localized community immunity, reducing downstream belief among neighbors. Defensive advisories attenuate victim conversion proportionally to institutional clarity and trust.

Responsible Use Policy

Standards & Research Ethics

Responsible Use Notice: Persona is an analytical research and defense tool designed to evaluate communication vulnerabilities and test defensive counter-messaging. It strictly prohibits the generation, optimization, or assistance in creating harmful, fraudulent, or deceptive messages. All simulation outputs reflect synthetic behavioral approximations under specified modeling assumptions and should not be interpreted as absolute real-world forecasts or deterministic predictions.
